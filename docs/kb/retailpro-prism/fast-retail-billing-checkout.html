Fast Retail Billing & Checkout FAQ

Retail Pro Prism features a highly customizable POS interface built on a web-based architecture — running in standard web browsers on desktop PCs, iPads and iPhones. This flexibility allows your team to provide fast billing anywhere in the store, from a fixed terminal to the sales floor.

How can we customize the UI to fit our brand?​

The Prism UI is designed for deep personalization without requiring complex coding. Through the UI Manager, a Retail Consultant can help you:

  • Change brand assets — update the UI with your company logo and custom background images.
  • Tailor layouts — configure "Zones" to show or hide specific buttons, such as tenders, lookup fields, or transaction options.
  • Adjust menus — customize the Transaction and Item menus so only the most relevant tools are visible to your cashiers.

How does Retail Pro Prism accelerate the checkout process?​

  • Advanced item lookup — find items instantly using ALUs, UPCs, descriptions, or partial wildcards.
  • Quick customer creation — add new customers "on the fly" directly from a transaction in progress.
  • Automated promotions — complex promotions apply automatically as soon as criteria are met.
  • Consolidated scanning — scanning the same item multiple times automatically consolidates it into a single line with updated quantity.

Can cashiers handle different transaction types at once?​

Yes. A single transaction can include a mixture of sale, return and order items — a customer can buy new shoes while returning a shirt and placing a special order, all in one seamless workflow.

What payment methods (tenders) are supported?​

Prism supports virtually any payment method: cash, checks, credit/debit cards, plus:

  • Central gift cards and store credit — real-time validation across all stores prevents fraud and "double-dipping."
  • Custom tenders — define up to 10 unique custom tenders for modern alternative payment apps or local billing requirements.

How does the system help prevent errors during checkout?​

  • Merchandise & customer images — item images let cashiers visually verify the correct product; customer images add security for high-value transactions or store credit redemptions.
  • Integrated EFT — integration with processors like Adyen or Global Payments automatically sends the "Amount Due" to the card terminal, eliminating manual entry errors.

Need help with layout design or workflow optimization? Raise a support ticket or contact your Retail Consultant.
