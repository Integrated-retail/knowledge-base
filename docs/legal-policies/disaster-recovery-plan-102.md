## Purpose

To minimize downtime and data loss in the event of a major incident affecting Integrated Retail's systems or a client's deployment.

## Typical elements

*   Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) targets per system criticality.
*   Documented failover procedures for critical services.
*   Regular testing of backup restoration (see Backup Policy).
*   Defined roles and communication plan during an incident.

Specific RTO/RPO targets are agreed per client deployment.
