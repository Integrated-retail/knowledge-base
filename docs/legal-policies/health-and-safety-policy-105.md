## Commitment

Integrated Retail is committed to providing a safe working environment for employees, contractors and visitors, in line with applicable local workplace safety regulations (e.g. Singapore's Workplace Safety and Health Act).

## Typical elements

*   Safe use of equipment during on-site installations (POS hardware, cameras, networking equipment).
*   Clear incident reporting procedures.
*   Regular review of workplace safety practices.
