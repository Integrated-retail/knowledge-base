Pilot / proof-of-concept engagements are typically governed by a lightweight agreement covering:

*   **Scope** — what is being tested, and for how long.
*   **Success criteria** — how the pilot will be evaluated.
*   **Data handling** — how any data used during the pilot is treated.
*   **Commercial terms** — whether the pilot is paid, and what happens at the end of the pilot period (convert to full contract, extend, or end).
