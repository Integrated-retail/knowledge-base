Integrated Retail routinely enters into Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) with clients and partners to protect confidential information shared during evaluations, implementations and ongoing engagements.

## Typical scope

*   Definition of what counts as "Confidential Information".
*   Obligations to protect and not disclose that information to third parties.
*   Exclusions (information already public, independently developed, etc.).
*   Duration of the confidentiality obligation.
