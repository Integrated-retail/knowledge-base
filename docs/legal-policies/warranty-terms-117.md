Integrated Retail provides a standard warranty period for supplied hardware and software, covering defects in materials or workmanship under normal use.

## What's typically covered

*   Repair or replacement of defective hardware within the warranty period.
*   Bug fixes for reported software defects.

## What's typically excluded

*   Damage from misuse, unauthorized modification, or third-party integrations.
*   Consumables and normal wear and tear.

Exact warranty duration and terms are specified in the product order form or contract.
