Integrated Retail supports the retailers and communities we work with across APAC by helping local businesses grow sustainably through better technology.

## Our focus areas

*   **Enabling local retail growth** — helping fashion, lifestyle and CPG retailers across Asia Pacific scale and compete.
*   **Responsible technology** — favoring solutions that reduce waste (e.g. digital processes over paper, efficient inventory management that reduces overstock).
*   **Community** — supporting the retail ecosystem through knowledge sharing, events and partnerships.

This statement can be expanded with specific CSR programs and metrics as they are formalized.
