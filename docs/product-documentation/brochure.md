# Brochure

Integrated Retail is a leading provider of advanced retail systems in the
Asia Pacific region, focused on fashion, lifestyle and consumer packaged
goods retailers. Our solutions power over 3,000 storefronts and facilitate
more than US$1 billion in commerce across physical and online stores.

With offices in Singapore, Indonesia and Thailand, we support retail
businesses across Asia through an on-ground, multi-lingual team — combining
software, infrastructure, consulting, implementation, support and business
transformation services.

## What we offer

- **POS Software** — omni-channel point-of-sale built for fashion, lifestyle
  and CPG retail.
- **E-commerce Integration** — connecting online and offline channels into a
  single, seamless shopping experience.
- **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)** — unified customer profiles and
  loyalty across every channel.
- **Inventory Management** — real-time stock visibility across stores and
  selling channels.
- **Reporting and Analytics** — see [Reporting](./reporting) for details.
- **Traffic Counting** — in-store footfall and conversion insight.
- **Conversational Commerce** and **AI-based Innovative Solutions**
- **Process Optimisation**
