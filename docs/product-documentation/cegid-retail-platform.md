# Cegid Retail Platform

Cegid is one of **Integrated Retail's two core technology platforms**
(alongside [Retail Pro Prism](./retailpro-prism-platform)). We partner with
**Cegid**, a global leader in retail technology, to deliver unified
commerce solutions for fashion, lifestyle and consumer goods retailers
across Asia Pacific.

Full official product documentation is maintained by Cegid at
**[retail-docs.cegid.cloud](https://retail-docs.cegid.cloud/)**. The
highlights below are written by Integrated Retail to help retailers
quickly see which capabilities matter most.

## Why retailers choose the Cegid platform

### Cegid Retail Y2 — Unified Commerce Platform
A modern POS and unified commerce platform that brings in-store,
e-commerce and mobile channels together — so stock, pricing and customer
data stay consistent everywhere a sale happens. Ideal for retailers
running (or moving to) an omnichannel model.

### Cegid Retail UR / Winstore — Unified Commerce Platform
An established unified commerce platform with deep retail functionality,
well suited to retailers with complex, multi-store operations that need a
proven, battle-tested POS and store management backbone.

### Cegid Orli / SCM — Fashion ERP & Planning
Purpose-built ERP for fashion and lifestyle retail: merchandising,
buying/planning, and supply chain — the tools a growing fashion brand
needs to plan collections and manage stock across a network of stores.

### Cegid Retail Intelligence — Retail Performance & Analytics
Turns POS and inventory data into actionable insight — sales performance,
stock health and customer behavior — so decisions across the business are
based on real, up-to-date numbers rather than gut feel.

## What this means for your business

Retailers evaluating a technology upgrade typically look at this platform
for:

- **One view of stock and sales** across every store and online channel.
- **Faster rollout** of new stores, powered by a platform already proven
  across 3,000+ storefronts globally.
- **Data-driven decisions** via built-in reporting and analytics, without
  bolting on a separate BI tool.
- **Room to grow** — from a single-brand fashion retailer to a
  multi-country, multi-brand operation.
