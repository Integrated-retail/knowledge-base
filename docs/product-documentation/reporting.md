# Reporting

Our Reporting and Analytics solution links information from every channel
into a single, holistic picture, so businesses can act quickly. Reporting is
designed to be easy and intuitive, with access from PC, mobile and other
devices — not just for head-office teams, but for staff across the retail
value chain.

This ties into our broader **Staff Productivity** tools: predictive
analytics, in-context alerts, one-point information updates and consolidated
views on execution across the retail chain.
