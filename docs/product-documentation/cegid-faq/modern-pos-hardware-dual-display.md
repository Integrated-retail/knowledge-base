At Integrated Retail, we specialize in retail technology deployment that
transforms the traditional checkout into a high-engagement touchpoint. By
leveraging hardware integration and the robust capabilities of Cegid
Retail Y2, we help brands implement modern POS displays that prioritize
customer transparency and operational excellence.

### How can dual display setups improve transparency at my checkout counters?

A dual-display setup — a primary cashier screen and a secondary
customer-facing display — is essential for building trust. The 2nd
display can show real-time transaction data: item descriptions,
quantities and tax amounts as they are scanned. When no transaction is in
progress, it can show a customized welcome or scrolling message.

### What customization options are available for customer-facing displays?

Store managers can:

- **Style the interface** — choose between standard text or a "Customer
  LCD display" look for a sleek, modern feel.
- **Optimize visibility** — adjust font colors and background themes for
  various lighting conditions.
- **Adapt hardware layouts** — for registers where the screen lies flat
  on the counter, a "Reverse display" feature inverts the text for the
  customer's perspective.

### Can I use the POS hardware to display digital marketing or web content?

Yes. Cashier touch-pad buttons can be configured to trigger specific
actions, including displaying a **web page** directly through the POS
interface — useful for promotional videos, digital catalogs, or loyalty
sign-up forms during checkout.

### What other modern hardware peripherals does Integrated Retail support?

Through specialized drivers, we support a full ecosystem of devices:

- **Integrated EFT terminals** — amount sent automatically to the payment
  terminal, reducing manual entry errors.
- **Advanced scanners** — 2D/QR code scanners for mobile coupons and
  digital loyalty cards.
- **Secure identification** — fingerprint readers and magnetic stripe
  badge readers for rapid staff authentication.
- **Official document readers** — for passports and identity cards, for
  tax-free shopping or high-value sales.

### How does Integrated Retail ensure the stability of these hardware integrations?

We use a **Device Manager** and specialized mapping tables to link your
Cegid software to physical hardware, assigning "triggers" so a single
register can drive multiple cash drawers or payment terminals based on
transaction type. CBR Network Control (CBRNC) monitors the communication
speed between hardware and the central database.

### What happens to my hardware if the store loses internet connectivity?

Standalone Mode keeps your hardware — including the customer display and
scanners — fully operational. Items can still be scanned using a local
database, and receipts print normally.
