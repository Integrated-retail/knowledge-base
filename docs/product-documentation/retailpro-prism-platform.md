# Retail Pro Prism Platform

Retail Pro Prism is one of **Integrated Retail's core technology
platforms** (alongside [Cegid](./cegid-retail-platform) and
[FootfallCam](./footfallcam-platform)). We partner with **Retail Pro**, a
specialty retail POS platform used by over 9,000 retailers and 54,000
stores in 130+ countries, to deliver flexible, highly-configurable store
systems for fashion, lifestyle and consumer
goods retailers across Asia Pacific.

Full official product documentation is maintained by Retail Pro at
**[my.retailpro.com](https://my.retailpro.com/)**. The highlights below
are written by Integrated Retail to help retailers quickly see which
capabilities matter most.

## Why retailers choose Retail Pro Prism

### Tailorable, touch-first POS
A fully customizable point-of-sale interface — available on Windows, iOS
and Android — built for fast checkout, with full functionality available
even when a store briefly loses connectivity (offline mode).

### Deep inventory & operations management
Replenishment, purchasing, receiving, transfers, inventory kits and
vendor management, alongside robust, rules-based pricing and promotions
that apply automatically at checkout.

### Customer management & reporting
Customer capture, purchase history and lookup at the register, plus
dynamic KPI reports and dashboards so store and head-office teams see the
same numbers.

### Configurable security & permissions
Employee and group management lets you define roles and permissions
precisely — down to which staff can apply a discount, process a return,
or access reporting.

## What this means for your business

Retailers evaluating Retail Pro Prism typically value:

- **Flexibility** — a modular, highly configurable platform that adapts
  to how your business actually operates, rather than forcing a fixed
  workflow.
- **Proven at scale** — trusted by specialty retail brands including
  Aesop, Puma, Oakley, Under Armour and Victorinox, across 130+ countries.
- **Global-ready** — multi-currency, multi-language and built-in support
  for regional tax requirements (VAT, GST and similar).
- **Room to grow** — advanced add-ons available as you need them,
  including RFID tracking, open-to-buy planning and visual analytics.
